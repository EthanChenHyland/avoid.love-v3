# avoid.love V3 — visual art bible

## Emotional direction

Intimate editorial romance under pressure from an overconfident clinical system. The opening frames feel measured, pale, cool, and bureaucratic; warmth, paper, red thread, flowers, handwriting, and human traces progressively contaminate that order until the system disappears.

## Image language

- Lighting: soft directional window or table-lamp light with deep, natural falloff; no glossy studio rim lights.
- Lens: 50–85 mm still-life intimacy, occasional close macro crop; shallow depth of field only when it strengthens hierarchy.
- Grain: fine photographic grain, restrained halation, slight paper/film age.
- Paper: warm ivory, cotton rag, receipts, tracing paper, worn folds, graphite and oxblood ink.
- Red: deep carmine/oxblood rather than candy red; warm rose enters only late.
- Flowers: pressed, bruised, half-dry, petal-level detail; avoid wedding bouquets and Valentine styling.
- Shadows: soft-edged but directional, physically plausible, with occasional foreground occlusion.
- Photographic age: contemporary image quality with archival handling marks rather than fake sepia nostalgia.
- Isolation: dark charcoal or warm paper backdrops that permit clean compositing and masking.
- Realism: tactile photographic realism with subtle surreal staging.
- Surrealism: physical impossibilities should feel fabricated by an art department, not rendered as fantasy imagery.

## Continuity objects

The same three objects should recur across chapters: a red thread, a pressed red flower/petal, and a folded handwritten note. They move from constrained evidence to residue, archive specimen, stretched distance marker, distraction clutter, containment input, void remnant, and finally free romantic material.
